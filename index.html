Take mode

One line at a time, with a timestamped take log you can turn into Resolve markers.

→ ←Turn. Next / previous line.
Short pressStart take. Press again to stop.
Long press, GMark the take good. Moves to the next line.
XMark the take scratch.
SSlate: white flash plus a sync point in the log.
LOpen the take log.

Script markup

  • # Hook on its own line marks a section. Sections show as amber cue labels and are jump targets.
  • [beat] or [smile] shows as a dim italic direction.
  • A blank line starts a new paragraph. In take mode each paragraph is one line to shoot, or each line if you choose.
  • Scripts and take logs are kept in this browser on this device.
